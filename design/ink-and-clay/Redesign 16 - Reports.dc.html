Decisions this brief didn’t cover:
1. Date range uses a segmented control (it’s a tab-like switch, so it keeps the tab shape). Owner, Template and Client are dropdown inputs. On tablet and phone they collapse into one “Filters” button that opens a sheet.
2. “Viewed” counts proposals that reached Viewed or any later stage, including declined and expired ones the client opened. “Open now” = Sent + Viewed.
3. Pipeline colors darken from Draft to Billing set up, and every segment carries its count, so the bar reads in grayscale. Exits use a hatched red pattern in their own block to the right.
4. Chart: Sent bars are outlined and hatched, Signed bars are solid, and every bar shows its value. The toggle switches the same card to a table (shown on tablet).
5. Tables sort by any column (▼ marks the active one). On phone they become cards with a Sort dropdown.
6. Sample data: 40 proposals. Stage counts add to 40, and template and owner rows add up to the headline numbers ($18,450 one-time, $2,140/mo).
