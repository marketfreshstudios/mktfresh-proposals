What changed and why:
1. Status first. Three health tiles under the title answer “is it working, and is anything waiting on me” before any detail. Problems link to the proposals they affect.
2. Actions in the header. One main button by state (Open Stripe / Reconnect QuickBooks / Connect). Reconnect no longer shows while healthy, and Disconnect moves into the ⋯ menu so it isn’t next to everyday buttons.
3. “How billing uses it” reads as steps, in plain words, with one “Edit in Billing defaults” link on the card instead of three repeated links.
4. Not connected is a real start page: what it does, then Connect. Stripe asks test or live first.
5. Jargon removed: “Configured for this deployment” becomes the account name and ID, “Webhooks” becomes “Billing events”, and “Not recorded” rows are hidden. Staff see who to ask instead of disabled buttons.

Decisions this brief didn’t cover:
1. The setup checklist splits into “Needed before you send” (brand, signatory, email sending, Stripe, QuickBooks) and optional steps. QuickBooks counts as needed but can be skipped by choosing to record invoice numbers by hand. Send stays blocked until the needed steps are done, matching the readiness check.
2. New workspaces get three sample templates with no prices, marked “Sample”. They’re ordinary templates that can be edited or deleted.
3. Once the needed steps are done, the checklist collapses into a one-line note (2b, top) that can be dismissed. Staff never see the checklist; they get a “What you can do” card instead (1c).
4. Every empty state has one sentence on what will appear, and at most two buttons. Empty tabs later on (no countersigns, no search results) use a small inline message, not the big illustration.
