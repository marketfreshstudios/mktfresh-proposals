2Editor panel cards, every tab
The card style applied to every tab. The panel ground is the cool ink tint #F4F7F7; every group is a white card (14px corners, 1px #DCE3E6). An open card gets a #B9C9CE border, a hairline under its header and a soft shadow. Closed cards show only their title, with no summary text.
Where a closed card shows something on the right, it's information you'd otherwise have to open it for: a line's price, the Readiness count, the number of saved blocks. Things that aren't collapsible (totals, the send card, notes) sit between cards without a header.
Sub-line placement: live puts “Sub-line” in the row's footer, next to Details, Remove and Done, so it reads as a row action and is far from the prices it adds to. I moved it to directly under the last sub-line, renamed it “Add sub-line”, and gave it a dashed outline, matching “Add from catalog or a custom line”. The footer keeps only Details, Remove and Done.
Content comes from quote-items-panel.tsx (Choose one, Plan terms, Billing mapping), field-options.tsx (groups Team, Job, Client, Proposal, Sender & workspace, Totals), block-options.ts (Text, Media, Proposal, Saved blocks) and proposal-styles.tsx.
2aQuote, one line open. Matches live quote-items-panel.tsx: name, description, the sub-line (Amount, Qty, Interval, Billing mapping), Optional, then the Optional switch and the Make default selection radio on one row, with a divider between them. Standalone optional lines use a checkbox with the same label. In closed rows, “Default” under the name marks the default choice; nothing else shows there. The row chevron sits on the right after the amount, as it does live.
