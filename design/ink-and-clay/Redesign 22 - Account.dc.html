1Your account · /account
Route: /account, opened from the account menu (1c). It isn’t in the main nav; the sidebar highlights nothing while it’s open. Each section is its own tab with its own URL (/account, /account/security, /account/notifications, /account/signature, /account/preferences). The nav sits on the left on desktop, as tabs across the top on tablet, and as a list of sub-pages on phone. Toggles save instantly with a “Saved · Undo” toast; text fields save on blur.
Decisions the brief didn’t cover:
1. Required alerts aren’t shown. “Client signed, needs countersign” and “Billing needs attention” always go to anyone who can countersign, so they don’t appear as settings. The page only lists what each person can change.
2. Signature: only the company signatory (Jonathan) draws a signature here, because every countersign shows his name. Staff see his signature read-only, with a note that the audit trail records who applied it (1b2).
3. Time zone is personal and only changes times you see. Client-facing dates (valid until, signed on) keep the workspace time zone, so the PDF never changes with the viewer.
4. Email change needs your password and a confirmation link to the new address; the old address gets a notice, and other sessions are signed out once it’s confirmed.
5. Two-step verification uses an authenticator app with 8 backup codes. A super admin can’t see codes, only whether it’s on.
