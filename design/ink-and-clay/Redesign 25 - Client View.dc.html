1Client view and Preview as client
Built from live client-proposal.tsx and proposal-questions.tsx: the header with brand and “Valid until”, the Review | Sign | Billing progress, the document, “Your choices” with totals, Ask a question and Review and sign, the Ask a question sheet (section, name and email when needed, a 2,000-character question) and “Your questions”. The client side uses the workspace brand (MarketFresh green, Source Serif 4), with cool neutrals and no beige.
Questions by page or block: 1) Every question button is the same dark round button, and it always straddles the edge of what it belongs to: half on the page or block, half outside it, so it reads as an app control and not as proposal content. 2) Desktop: one sits on the top-right corner of each page (“Ask about this page”), and hovering a section or block highlights it and shows one on that block’s right edge (“Ask about this paragraph”). 3) Phone: there’s no room for the button to hang off the side of a block, so it sits inside instead. One sits on the bottom edge of the header (a general question), and tapping a block highlights it with the button inside its right edge. All are icon-only, with an aria-label and a tooltip, and phone buttons are 44px. 3) The sheet or popover names what the question is about, with Change. 4) “Your questions” sits under the sign box: each question shows what it’s about and its status. Edit and Delete are offered until it gets a reply, then View reply.
Needs the API: live questions accept section only. Block questions need block_id and a short excerpt saved with the thread, so staff see the same context on the record.
Preview as client is the same page under an ink staff bar: Back to editor, “Preview, not sent”, a Reading | Signing | Billing step switch, Desktop | Phone, and Send. Question buttons still open the sheet, but Send question is disabled with “Preview: questions aren’t sent from here.”
