4Proposal details | every state
Every state of the proposal details page. Details runs across the page under the hero. Below it, the main column holds what needs action and the agreement, and a 340px column holds Engagement and the Timeline. Saved edits go live in the client view right away, so there’s no “changes not sent” state. Drafts open in the editor, so they don’t have a details page. The hero is always the place to find the status, who acts next and the one action. The sections below it show or hide depending on the state:
Client questions: only while the client has asked at least one. Billing (status and section): only when the proposal bills through Stripe or QuickBooks. Documents: only after both signatures. Engagement: an empty state before the first open, hidden for imports. Edit: only while the proposal is unsigned and not imported.
