# Starter proposal templates

Approved by the owner on 6 Nov 2026. This file is the source wording for the seven starter templates (Template 7 approved on 7 Nov 2026). Codex: use this wording exactly, turn it into template blocks, and ask in the PR if a block type doesn't exist.

## Rules for every template

- Section order: Cover → Overview → Goals → Scope and deliverables → Timeline and what we need from you → Why {{workspace.name}} (with case studies) → Investment (pricing table) → Next steps → Terms (contract clauses) → Signatures. Target 5–8 pages.
- Each `**Bold label**` line below is a section heading (Heading 1) unless it says otherwise. Bullets are list blocks; tables are table blocks (use the existing table block; if there isn't one, ask).
- Text in `[square brackets]` is guidance for staff. It stays visible in the editor, styled as a placeholder if the editor has a way to do that.
- Templates hold no prices. The Investment section is the existing `pricing_table` block.
- The terms are plain-language starting points, not legal advice. The Templates screen shows a one-line note: "Starter terms are a starting point, not legal advice. Have an attorney review them before you use them."
- Case studies stay generic placeholders for now (owner decision); MarketFresh's real ones come later through the connector.
- The audit fee credit is not built in (owner decision).

## Terms clauses (full wording)

No [bracketed guidance] in the terms, so staff don't have to edit them on every proposal; they change the wording in the template instead.

Contract block, heading "Terms". Number them as shown; a template that leaves a clause out keeps the remaining numbers in order (renumber).

1. **Scope of work.** We'll deliver the work described in this proposal. Anything not listed is out of scope. If you'd like to change the scope, we'll agree the change and any cost in writing before we start it.
2. **Your responsibilities.** You'll give us the content, access and feedback we need on time. If something we need is late, the timeline moves by the same amount.
3. **Payment.** One-time fees are invoiced and due within 14 days. Recurring fees are billed monthly in advance. If a payment is more than 15 days late, we may pause work until it's paid.
4. **Timeline and delays.** Dates in this proposal are estimates. Neither of us is responsible for delays caused by things outside our reasonable control.
4.4 **Own It Over Time.** Keep the existing clause text from seed/marketfresh.ts exactly as it is (owner confirmed it is current), with its existing show-when rule.
5. **Ownership.** Once you've paid in full, you own the finished work we create for you. We keep the right to use our general tools, methods and know-how, and we may show the work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.
6. **Third-party services.** Hosting, domains, plugins and other services from other companies have their own terms and fees. We're not responsible for their outages or changes.
7. **Confidentiality.** We'll each keep the other's private business information private, and use it only for this work.
8. **Warranty and liability.** We'll fix defects in our work that you report within 30 days of delivery, at no charge. Apart from that, our total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid us in the 3 months before the claim.
9. **Term and cancellation.** Monthly services continue month to month until either of us cancels with 30 days' written notice. Fees already paid aren't refunded.
10. **Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of {{workspace.state}}.
11. **Entire agreement.** This signed proposal is the whole agreement between us about this work and replaces earlier discussions.

## Business state (owner decision for clause 10)

## How they're added

- New workspaces get all seven on creation, active, in the order 6, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 (General first; Social media after Monthly marketing retainer). Default quotes only in the MarketFresh seed.
- MarketFresh (existing workspace): add the six once, idempotently (keyed by a stable starter key, never by name), never overwriting anything. The old "Website proposal sample" is renamed "Website proposal sample (old)" and hidden from new proposals, not deleted.
- Readiness: a proposal can't be sent while any text still contains `[bracketed guidance]`; the message names each section to fix. Don't flag brackets inside field chips, links or prices.
- Templates screen: each starter shows its one-line description.
