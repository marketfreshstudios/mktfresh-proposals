Quiet paper, confident type, one brand color.

Editorial, not admin. Serif headlines and documents, a warm neutral ground, hairlines instead of boxes, and almost no shadow.
The workspace owns one color. Its accent drives buttons, links, focus and selection. Status colors are fixed by the product and never change with the brand.
The document is always paper. What a client reads stays white in dark mode so it matches the sealed PDF. Everything around it follows the theme.
Built from what exists. Same token names as the repo’s design system; values updated. Components map one to one to React components (names in mono).

Logo (SVG or PNG, shown at 28px high on phone, 36px on desktop). Without a logo, the workspace name is shown in the header. Never a “powered by”.
Accent: any color with at least 4.5:1 contrast against white text. Below that, Settings shows the failing ratio and suggests the nearest darker shade of the same hue.
#E0A83A is 2.1:1 with white. Try #8A5E12.
