Build spec

“Sign” from the list opens the proposal; it never signs in place

A countersignature is a legal act. The person needs the client-signed document, the client’s signature and consent, the company signature preview and the on-behalf statement in front of them. The list only gets them there in one tap.

Where it appearsList row (desktop, tablet), card (phone), ⋯ menu, Home “Countersign” group, client page notification email.Only when status === 'won' && !company_signed and the viewer is in company_signatory.authorized_user_ids. Otherwise show the “Needs countersign” tag instead, no button.
What it doesA plain link (<Link>, not a button with a handler) to /proposals/:id?action=countersign. Row click and the Sign link are separate targets; the link stops propagation.The record page reads the param, renders normally, then opens CountersignSheet (dialog on desktop and tablet, bottom sheet on phone) and moves focus to its heading. Closing the sheet removes the param with router.replace.
What the sheet must show1. Client-signed, frozen document summary: chosen items, both totals, version and hash.2. Client signature image or typed name, name, title, email, timestamp.3. Company signature preview: stored image, “Jonathan Garces”, title, today’s date.4. If the viewer is not the signatory: the on-behalf statement checkbox naming the viewer.5. Consent text in full with its checkbox. 6. “Countersign as Jonathan Garces”, disabled with a reason until both boxes are ticked.
GuardsSend revision and reviewed_hash with POST /api/proposals/:id/countersign. On 409, show the stale banner in the sheet and block submit.If already countersigned when the page loads (someone else did it), ignore the param and show a toast: “Franz already countersigned this at 4:31 PM.”AI connectors never see this action.
After successSheet shows “Sealing the final copy…”, closes, record updates to Countersigned or Won. If the user came from the list, “Back to proposals” returns to the same view and scroll position; the row no longer shows Sign.
Try it: press Sign on Brightline Foods above. It opens the record (3a) with the countersign sheet already open.
