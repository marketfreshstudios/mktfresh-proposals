1Information architecture · Client signing flow on phone
MarketFresh Proposals · Redesign

From “client asked for X” to “proposal sent” in minutes, and from “opened the email” to “signed and paid” in four taps.

This first pass covers the navigation model and the full client flow on a 375px phone. The editor (desktop and phone) comes next, then the record, dashboard, the full design system and edge states at all three widths.

What I kept and what I changed
  • Kept the repo's rules: two totals always, fixed status words, paper-white document, frozen after signing, AI never sends or signs, nothing auto-reloads.
  • Kept Source Serif 4 for the document and IBM Plex Mono for evidence. Replaced Public Sans with Instrument Sans for UI, and moved from cool grey to warm paper neutrals so the product reads editorial, not admin.
  • Money in compact UI reads “$85/mo”; in sentences and the PDF it reads “$85/month”.
  • Sample client is Brightline Foods (Rowan Vale). Products and amounts come from the seed catalog and are illustrative.
