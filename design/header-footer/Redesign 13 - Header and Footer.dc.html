Decisions the brief didn’t cover
1. File name. Saved as Redesign 13, since Redesign 12 is already Studio v2.
2. Band position. The band sits just inside the top or bottom margin, with a fixed 0.25 in gap before the content. Heights are 0.4, 0.5 (default) or 0.75 in.
3. Hiding keeps the space. “Hide on this page” leaves an empty band of the same height, so hiding one header never moves page breaks. Turning Header off in Styles removes it from every page and gives the space back.
4. Cover default: agreed. The cover starts without a header and footer. A “Use shared header” pill appears in the cover’s top margin on hover or focus, and Styles has a “Show on cover” switch.
5. When the anchor block is deleted. A unique band moves to the block that now starts the page. If the page itself disappears, the band is dropped, and a toast offers Undo.
6. Reset to template resets only the shared header and footer. Pages with their own keep them. “Use shared on every page” is a separate action.
7. Page fields ({{ tokPN }}, {{ tokPC }}) only appear in the Fields list while a header or footer is being edited. In body text they would be ambiguous.
8. Controls live in the margin. Labels, Edit, ⋯ and the edit bar sit in the page margin: above the header and below the footer. They never take space from content or touch the gutter + or the AI ✓.
