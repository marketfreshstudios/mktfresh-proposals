1Studio v3 · Yeswell branding, roles, settings and tablet
Patterns carried over
Two kinds of page. List pages (Templates, Emails, Products, Connections, Workspace, Activity) sit in the normal app shell. Anything you edit (a template or an email) opens full-screen in the editor shell: one header bar, a canvas, and a collapsible right panel with tabs.
One primary action per page, as a dark pill, top right. Everything else is a quiet outline button or a text link.
Changes to how it works
Emails are edited in place. Today there's a form on the left and a separate preview on the right. Now you type straight into the email as the client will see it, using the same floating edit bar as proposals. Fields show as chips, and a switch shows real values from a sample client or a chosen proposal.
Emails are ordered by when they go out (Send, Follow up, Sign, Billing) rather than in one flat list, so it's clear what a client receives at each step.
New in v3. Yeswell branding on the app chrome (the workspace’s own brand stays on proposals and emails). Team roles are Super admin and Staff, and Claude lives in Connections. Three editable staff emails (Team and jobs). Workspace adds Proposal styles, Billing defaults and Email sending, plus the “Powered by Yeswell” switch in Brand. Templates get Default quote · Fields · Blocks · Styles, with header and footer defaults in Styles. Stripe shows its actual mode. Tablet frame 1g.
Workspace becomes one page with a section list on the left, like Sections in the editor, instead of one long scroll.
1aStudio · desktop 1440 · clickable. Use the left nav. Click an email or a template to open its editor, and a product to open its details.
